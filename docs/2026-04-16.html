## Models & Research This week's research signal cuts through the benchmark arms race with a more substantive question: not whether models *can* reason, but whether they reason *correctly* in compositional, tool-augmented environments where failure is measurable. Anthropic's simultaneous push on Claude Code and autonomous "Routines" marks a concrete architectural bet that the next capability frontier is agentic orchestration, not raw benchmark scores. Meanwhile, the arXiv papers below reflect a field increasingly focused on patching specific failure modes — grounding, spatial geometry, segmentation of novel entities — rather than chasing general capability claims.
- **[Hugging Face / IBM Research] VAKRA benchmark reveals systematic failure modes in enterprise AI agents** — IBM Research's VAKRA measures compositional reasoning across chained API calls in enterprise-like environments, explicitly distinguishing tool-selection errors from reasoning errors; early leaderboard results (linked in the release) show frontier models failing disproportionately on multi-hop tool dependencies rather than isolated tasks. [Link](https://huggingface.co/blog/ibm-research/vakra-benchmark-analysis) - **[VentureBeat] Anthropic ships redesigned Claude Code desktop app (Mac/Windows) plus "Routines" in research preview, released April 14, 2026** — "Routines" is the more consequential piece: it allows Claude Code to execute multi-step, scheduled workflows autonomously, a direct architectural challenge to GitHub Copilot Workspace and the emerging agentic IDE category. [Link](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/we-tested-anthropics-redesigned-claude-code-desktop-app-and-routines-heres-what-enterprises-should-know) - **[arXiv] ROSE (Retrieval-Oriented Segmentation Enhancement) proposes injecting up-to-date retrieval into multimodal segmentation models** — The paper introduces NEST (Novel Emerging Segmentation Task), formally defining the problem of segmenting entities absent from a model's training distribution; this is a direct critique of LISA and similar MLLM-based segmentation architectures that freeze world knowledge at pretraining cutoff. [Link](http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14147v1) - **[arXiv] SpatialEvo identifies a self-reinforcement failure mode in self-evolving spatial reasoning models** — The core finding: consensus-based pseudo-label construction causes models to encode rather than correct their own geometric errors during self-improvement loops, a structural problem for any self-play or self-distillation approach applied to 3D scene understanding.
